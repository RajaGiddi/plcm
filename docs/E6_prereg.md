# Pre-Registration: E6 — Drift Anatomy on HAR (Disease B Diagnostic)

**Status:** **v2, locked.** Seven review catches applied (§8).

**Scope correction from v1:** this is **3 training runs + analysis**, not
analysis-only. The audit (§0) found the HAR OFF arm had no checkpoints.

**Question:** Is HAR's adapter-resistant forgetting carried by encoder drift
*within the readout-relevant subspace* (in-S drift) — and is that drift explained
by internal absorption of the input shift?

**Program context:** Disease A (input-distribution interference) is solved and its
cure characterized. Disease B is hypothesized as in-S drift from the encoder
absorbing shifts internally. E6 measures whether Disease B exists as hypothesized,
its size, and its fingerprint — **before** any cure is attempted. Cure contract
(E7) only if this confirms the target.

### 0a. Interpretation lock for the regenerated OFF arm

E6's OFF anatomy is computed on the **new runs' own trajectories** — θ₀ and θ₄
come from a single execution, so the anatomy is internally self-consistent whether
or not the finals reproduce `e5_har_noadapt_*`.

Their finals are printed **beside** the originals and the spread reported as a

## 1. Measurements

Per arm × seed {42, 1337, 2024}, on HAR task-0 test inputs, θ₀ = end-of-task-0,
θ₄ = end-of-task-4:

- **M1 — in-S drift ratio (headline):** `drift_S(ON) / drift_S(OFF)`, E4b's
  H-SUB2 object. MNIST reference: **ratio 0.214** (from `drift_S` ON 0.1893,
  OFF 0.8849). *Arm-relative by design: HAR's absolute drift magnitudes may differ
  from MNIST's for reasons that are not the disease (sequence length, channel
  count), and the ratio nets those out.*
- **M2 — in-S energy fraction** `‖P_S·δ‖²/‖δ‖²` vs the random-subspace baseline
  and the S-energy share (E4b's "is the drift aimed?" test).
- **M3 — per-task-boundary decomposition (absorption fingerprint):** δ recomputed
  across each consecutive pair (θ_k → θ_{k+1}), attributing in-S drift to
  individual transitions.
- **M4 — classifier co-adaptation control:** repeat M1 with S from θ₄'s classifier
  instead of θ₀'s; report the principal angle between the two row spaces.
- **M5 — MNIST side-by-side:** the same table for the E4 MNIST arms, printed
  adjacent. The claim is comparative; the comparison lives in one table.

## 2. Hypotheses (fixed before any projection is computed)

- **H-B1 (Disease B exists on HAR):** ON/OFF `drift_S` ratio **≥ 0.43**
  (= 2 × MNIST's 0.214) — drift is unsteered relative to the regime where
  adapters work. MNIST's OFF-arm 0.885 bounds it above.
- **H-B2 (dominant channel):** v1-ON in-S energy fraction **≥ 1.0×** the S-energy
  share — drift *over*-represented in S, the opposite sign of MNIST's 0.44×
  under-representation.
- **H-B3′ (absorption fingerprint — grouped):** mean in-S increment over tasks
  **{3,4} ≥ 2× mean over {1,2}** (OFF arm).
  *Derivation:* row-0 damage averages **0.665** for {3,4} vs **0.038** for {1,2},
  a ~10:1 ratio, so demanding 2× on the drift increments is a real-effect gate.
  *(v1's ρ ≥ 0.5 on n=4 was a one-in-five null pass, and tasks 3/4 are near-tied
  at 0.688/0.643 — their relative rank is a coin flip. The grouped form treats
  that tie as the tie it is.)* **Spearman ρ still reported, descriptively.**
- **H-B4 (not reader-walk):** M4's principal angle is small enough that H-B1's
  verdict is identical under either S definition. If verdicts flip, classifier
  co-adaptation is a live channel and is **reported as such — not absorbed into
  the drift story.**
